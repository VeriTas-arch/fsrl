# Liu support-topology transport v1

## Registered outcome

`LIU_STRUCTURAL_MECHANISM_TRANSPORTED`

All three prospectively selected alternative graphs pass all eight registered
links independently in all three mandatory development backbones. This is
`9/9` graph-by-backbone cells and `72/72` link decisions, with no participant
pooling across graphs or networks and no across-network majority rule.

The result supports this model-side statement within the registered scope:

> A frozen meta-learned plastic network constructs competent, coherent, stable,
> individualized Liu-style rankings across matched sparse support graphs using
> a stable asymmetric computation: interacting `P_T` supports remote/global
> relational assembly, whereas exact edge state `a_T` preserves direct
> experience-specific fidelity without becoming a second transitive learner.

This is a development transport result across three fixed topologies and three
frozen backbones. It is not a network-population prevalence estimate, proof
that the exact graph identities were absent from the procedural meta-training
distribution, item-count or sparsity generalization, a unique implementation,
or a human/biological mechanism.

### Constructive global structure

The intact system remains competent on both observed and unobserved pairs.
Its pair field is almost entirely Hodge-additive, exact winner fields are
almost fully transitive, and Hodge orders remain positively aligned with true
rank. Relation LOO on the `a`-off branch has robust disjoint and third-party
effects in every cell. Thus `P_T` does not merely replay a fixed set of original
Liu pair labels; it constructs remote relational consequences under the three
alternative support connectivities.

### Stable individualization

The coherent field does not collapse to one common ranking. Mean pairwise
Kendall tau among eligible non-correct Hodge orders ranges from .430 to .546,
and the prevalence of at least one 80-percent-stable within-subject error ranges
from .893 to .948. Both registered diversity/stability boundaries pass in
every cell.

This individualization is model-internal and arises under the frozen stable
subject-encoding distribution. It does not establish that humans use that
encoding bottleneck.

### Functional asymmetry

Removing `a_T` causes a positive learned-pair probability loss in every cell;
the smallest lower confidence bound is still positive (`.00088`). With `P_T`
removed and `a_T` intact, learned probability remains above chance while
nonlearned probability stays at or below chance and remote LOO influence
collapses below one quarter of the intact global effect. Conversely, the
`a`-off global field retains robust nonlearned competence and remote
reassembly.

The high-hub graph has weaker nonlearned performance, Hodge-to-true alignment,
and local direct benefit than the two less concentrated graphs. This is
descriptive quantitative topology dependence, not failure of the registered
functional division. The result supports transport of computation, not metric
invariance across graphs.

### Exact local algorithm

In the repaired common-float64 estimand, the maximum tensor-state error is
`4.44e-16` and the maximum all-query Gram-read error is
`9.77e-15`. Therefore the existing exact algorithm transports unchanged:

`a_(t+1) = a_t + s_t^L e_(r_t)`,

`ell = K a_T`.

This is an exact representation of the frozen tensor trace; it does not show
that the tensor-product address or Gram kernel is unique.

## Preserved attempt-1 execution repair

Attempt 1 produced `TOPOLOGY_DEPENDENT_OR_UNRESOLVED` solely because it
compared a sequential float32 GPU trace with a grouped float64 Gram
reconstruction. Balanced and moderate-hub query discrepancies were
`1.312e-6` and `1.243e-6`, narrowly above the frozen `1e-6` threshold, while
the high-hub value was `8.171e-7`. These graph-constant values were identical
across all three backbones.

The attempt is preserved in
`results/liu_support_topology_transport_v1.attempt1.json`. Repair 1 changed
only the exact-compression estimator to compare the original tensor sum and
edge-ledger/Gram sum in the same float64 representation, matching the already
established exact-`a_T` estimand. GPU-versus-exact discrepancies remain in the
final artifact as diagnostics. The rerun's nonrepair projection is exactly
equal to attempt 1; a single changed model, behavior, field, LOO, bootstrap, or
secondary value would have invalidated the repair.

## Secondary behavior and limitations

Across the nine cells, sampled learned accuracy is `.922--.950`, sampled
nonlearned accuracy is `.716--.808`, symbolic-distance slope is
`.0455--.0516`, and the serial-position endpoint contrast is `.0553--.0840`.
Alternative-graph values are not calibrated to a human target because humans
were not tested on these graphs.

The original-graph excessive symbolic-distance slope, weak serial-position
endpoint, and seed-2104 self-inconsistency mismatch remain known limitations.
They were neither repair targets nor acceptance gates. This experiment does
not license temperature, `W_out`, `P_T`, `a_T`, evidence-admission, or
graph-specific tuning.

## Theory update and next test

The original Liu support graph is no longer the only graph instance supporting
the working mechanism. The supported model chain is now:

`matched Liu-style sparse evidence -> different admission rules ->`

- `P_T -> coherent, individualized, remote/global relational assembly`, and
- `a_T -> query-addressed direct-experience fidelity`.

The next Liu internal-validity axis is presentation order while holding graph,
support count, exposure count, item count, evidence, and model fixed. Freeze
blockwise-random, relation-clustered, and reverse-order schedules before
execution. Sparsity follows only after presentation order; item count follows
only after sparsity. List linking, classic transitive inference, Miconi
ancestry, MEG, and human-mechanism validation remain deferred.
