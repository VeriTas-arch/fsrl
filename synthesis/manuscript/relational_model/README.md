## Five-minute model

### 1. Behavioral phenomenon

Sparse, partially retained pairwise evidence can still produce coherent,
stable, and individualized relational judgments. On the frozen Liu-aligned
map, fresh networks 2104 and 2105 quantitatively reproduce six of nine
registered phenomena. The other three retain the correct qualitative effect
but remain quantitative mismatches: symbolic-distance dependence is too
steep, the serial-position endpoint contrast is too weak, and network 2104
overproduces self-inconsistent rankings.

The result is behavioral competence with explicit limits, not a perfect human
fit.

### 2. Computational tension

A single global ordering is useful for inference over unseen pairs, but can
redistribute or discard relation-specific evidence. A pure edge store
preserves direct observations, but does not assemble their remote relational
consequences. The current model combines these roles within one frozen system.

### 3. Current unified model

| Component | Computational role | Exact boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structural parameters `theta` | Meta-learn a recurrent update rule on generic ranking tasks | Fixed during each episode and throughout frozen Liu evaluation |
| Global state `P_T` | Interacting fast weights assemble remote, nonlearned, nearly potential-like structure from selectively admitted evidence | Necessary for episode-specific global assembly; not an identified biological state or an exact Bayesian updater |
| Local state `L_T` / ledger `a_T` | A query-addressed trace preserves broader directly experienced evidence | `a_T` is an exact coordinate representation of `L_T`, not a third state or a second transitive learner |

At query, one fixed readout combines the global margin from `P_T` with the local
correction reconstructed from `a_T`. The asymmetry is therefore functional:
history-dependent global assembly plus additive direct fidelity.

### 4. Frozen training-to-evaluation timeline

1. Meta-train the recurrent backbone on generic connected eight-item sparse
   graphs; prospectively exclude the Liu graph and its rank reflection.
2. Freeze the backbone and adapt only the positive local gain on new generic
   episodes. The local address and update rule remain fixed.
3. Jointly lock checkpoints, gains, evidence-admission equations, schedules,
   and analysis rules before exposing fresh networks 2104 and 2105 to the Liu
   evaluation.
4. Evaluate without Liu-stage fitting, participant pooling, network pooling,
   checkpoint selection, or threshold movement.

The fixed choice temperature is an inherited development calibration, not a
fresh-network confirmation fit. Sequential parameter fitting also does not
mean sequential runtime construction: `P_t` and `L_t` receive their respective
inputs during the same support episode.

### 5. Causal reading

Turning off `P_T` preserves a local learned-pair advantage while remote and
nonlearned performance collapses. Turning off the local contribution restores
the global-only logits exactly while removing the direct-fidelity benefit.
Evidence-routing and query-address derangements establish specificity. These
controls identify distinct model computations; they do not identify human
neural mechanisms.

## Claim cards

### Behavioral competence

- **Current claim:** the frozen system captures the main group and individual
  relational phenotype while preserving direct and inferred judgments.
- **Strongest positive evidence:** both fresh networks reproduce six of nine
  registered phenomena quantitatively; none of the other three is absent.
- **Retained negative evidence:** distance slope is too steep, endpoint
  contrast is too weak, and one network has excess self-inconsistent rankings.
- **Scope:** two fresh networks, with uncertainty assessed within each network;
  this is not a network-population prevalence estimate.
- **Cannot establish:** a perfect human fit or a human neural mechanism.
- **Trace:** [behavioral reproduction map](../../../studies/behavior_reproduction_map/README.md).

### Global assembly

- **Current claim:** the recurrent fast-weight state `P_T` is causally necessary
  for episode-specific remote and nonlearned relational assembly.
- **Strongest positive evidence:** reset, write-off, sensitivity-off,
  relation-LOO, factor-swap, and placement controls establish remote causal
  reach and history-dependent assembly.
- **Retained negative evidence:** one formal direction criterion is
  heterogeneous, and the tested potential, scalar-history, item-history, and
  full-state linear reductions are insufficient.
- **Scope:** the terminal field is nearly potential-like; that output geometry
  does not prove simple update dynamics or irreducibility.
- **Cannot establish:** exact sequential Bayesian updating, a unique compact
  algorithm, or a biological dopamine/fast-weight implementation.
- **Trace:** [global mechanism stage](../../../workflows/relational_model/README.md#3-global-assembly-mechanism).

### Local direct fidelity

- **Current claim:** broader evidence admission to the query-addressed local
  path improves fidelity for directly presented relations while leaving the
  global mechanism intact.
- **Strongest positive evidence:** independent replication and fresh-backbone
  confirmation show direct rescue, routing specificity, address specificity,
  and a `P`-off/local-off causal double dissociation.
- **Retained negative evidence:** the local path causes small bounded cross-talk
  on retained evidence and does not repair the global distance-slope mismatch.
- **Scope:** `L_T` and `a_T` are exactly equivalent representations of one
  additive local computation; the confirmed implementation is sufficient, not
  unique.
- **Cannot establish:** a second biological memory store or a measured human
  evidence-encoding variable.
- **Trace:** [fresh-backbone confirmation](../../../studies/dual_evidence_access_confirmation/README.md).

### Transport and generalization

- **Current claim:** the global/local functional division transports across
  registered topology, presentation-order, and item-count interventions; most
  functional links persist across evidence density, whose parent outcome
  remains unresolved.
- **Strongest positive evidence:** every registered link passes across three
  matched topologies, three schedules, and `N=6,8,10`; density transport passes
  23 of 24 parent cells.
- **Retained negative evidence:** the prediction that denser evidence reduces
  dependence on `P_T` is rejected, one density cell remains unresolved, and
  global quantitative quality degrades as item count grows.
- **Scope:** these are separate one-factor interventions, not a factorial
  generalization experiment.
- **Cannot establish:** arbitrary-graph transport, arbitrary timing or density,
  or scale-invariant performance.
- **Trace:** [algorithmic form and transport](../../../workflows/relational_model/README.md#6-algorithmic-form-and-transport).
