## Scientific role

**Question.** Does the global-only v1 mechanism jointly reproduce the registered Liu behavioral conjunction across unfiltered seeds?

**Finding.** All ten formal networks remained competent and preserved major causal and geometric positives, but every network failed the full behavioral conjunction with a shared excessive-distance-slope and learned-accuracy fingerprint.

**Claim boundary.** The common negative motivates a missing direct-local mechanism; it does not erase global reassembly or authorize seed filtering, temperature tuning, or checkpoint selection.
