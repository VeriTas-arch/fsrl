## Scientific role

**Question.** Can repeated-evidence plasticity stabilization improve complete internal
order formation and ranking composition in the two-scalar Resampled score learner?

experience-dependent Resampled learner. It preserves the codebook, information boundary,
score state, readout and generic objective; adds only a diminishing efficacy state; and
requires a nonbalanced schedule control before any relation-specific interpretation. No
execution or model promotion is registered.

**Claim boundary.** Design authority only. The candidate is motivated but untested.
