## Scientific role

**Question.** Can one fixed joint training distribution teach the unchanged single-P
affine RNN history-dependent relational revision, and can a persistent-hidden nonplastic
GRU learn the same behavior from identical information?

**Finding.** Three joint-trained plastic RNNs retain competence, adaptation and partial
outlier recovery but fail history-dependent crossover and preservation; all GRUs fail
competence. Fixed-budget candidate closed; conditional directional intervention not
launched.

**Claim boundary.** Three development pairs, fixed budget, synthetic revision and
generic retention only. No human fit, successful Liu phenotype retention, strict MF/MB
claim, optimal RL comparison, independent confirmation, minimality or main-model
promotion presumed.

## Provenance rule

Files under `records/` are byte-preserving relocations. Their former paths,
hashes, sizes, and source ref are recorded in `study.toml` and the global
migration map. New interpretation belongs in this capsule or `synthesis/`;
the frozen records themselves are not rewritten.
Commands and relative links inside a frozen report describe its historical
checkout. Use the maintained workflow for current commands, or the snapshot
replay guide for an exact detached-worktree replay.

Add a `figures/` directory only when this study has a promoted, reproducible
study-level figure. Cross-study paper figures belong in `synthesis/figures/`.
