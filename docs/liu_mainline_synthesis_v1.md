# Liu model mainline synthesis v1

## Bottom line

The Liu model mainline is now complete at the current model-level boundary.
The evidence no longer supports merely “good behavior on one fixed graph.” It
supports a stable asymmetric computation across prospectively frozen,
one-factor variations of the Liu-style task:

```text
displayed signed relational evidence
        |
        +-- selective global admission --> P_T --> coherent remote/nonlearned assembly
        |
        +-- broader weak local admission -> a_T --> query-addressed direct fidelity
```

The four transport studies do not form a full factorial design, and they do
not establish human neural implementation. Within that boundary, the
`P_T/a_T` division is now the working algorithm rather than a candidate tied to
the original eight-item support graph.

## What the model now explains

The positive chain is:

1. The task supplies item identity and signed metric support evidence without
   learning-stage choice or feedback.
2. Stable subject-specific encoding admits a selective subset into the global
   recurrent computation, while the confirmed v2.4 rule allows broader weak
   observed evidence into the local trace.
3. `P_T` constructs a near-additive, coherent relational potential. Its causal
   contribution reaches disjoint and third-party pairs, and removing it
   collapses nonlearned inference.
4. `a_T` is an exactly compressible antisymmetric edge ledger. Its natural
   query address selectively improves directly experienced relations; removing
   it restores the global-only model exactly.
5. The two paths jointly produce coherent but individualized rankings, stable
   pair-specific errors, direct learned fidelity, and nonlearned inference.
6. The division survives changes in graph topology, presentation schedule,
   observed-edge density, and the registered item-count range.

The strongest defensible model-level claim is:

> A meta-learned plastic recurrent network can transform sparse, partially
> encoded signed evidence into coherent yet individualized Liu-style rankings
> through two causally distinct expression routes: a distributed global
> constructor and a query-addressed direct-evidence trace.

## Negative constraints that remain active

The complete evidence does not justify declaring the Liu task solved without
qualification.

- The original frozen behavioral map reproduces six of nine phenomena
  quantitatively. Symbolic-distance slope is too steep, serial-position
  endpoint contrast is too weak, and one network overproduces inconsistent
  rankings.
- Density does not make the global path less necessary. Global dependence
  tends to increase with additional compatible evidence, while local
  all-query dependence remains near zero even though direct learned benefit is
  positive.
- Denser evidence progressively regularizes individualized orders. This is a
  supported continuum, not permission to erase the registered E=10 sparsity
  failure.
- At larger N, the causal organization persists but nonlearned accuracy,
  Hodge-order alignment, and remote-effect magnitude decline, while normalized
  distance slope rises. Mechanism transport is therefore not performance
  invariance.
- The studies use one-factor transport. They do not establish that every
  topology × order × density × size combination will pass.

These negatives revise the research direction toward global-policy scaling and
allocation only if a new model-side question is opened. They do not weaken the
confirmed `P_T/a_T` division or motivate tuning the frozen results.

work to one intra-op and one inter-op thread; the read-only sparsity
localization is CPU-only and was run with BLAS/OMP thread limits of one.

## Current stop/go boundary

The Liu structural-generalization program requested for the current model is
complete. Preserve all successful and failed registered outcomes. Do not add
post-hoc graphs, orders, densities, sizes, temperature changes, or retraining
to make the remaining quantitative mismatches disappear.

Human mechanism validation, MEG alignment, list linking, classic transitive
inference, Miconi ancestry, and new compression remain separate future layers.
If work continues only on the model, the next stage should start from a new,
explicit scientific question—most naturally the cardinality-sensitive global
policy—rather than another unregistered Liu variation.
