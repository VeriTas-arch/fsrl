## Scientific role

**Question.** Can a single affine scalar m=u^T h+c replace the two-tanh opponent
modulation head while retaining learned relational competence, individualized
organization and A/C compensation? What modulation and write opportunities do the frozen
reference networks actually use?

**Finding.** All three matched pairs support affine-head behavioral replacement: 36
competent cells, 15 Ce endpoints noninferior, Ae core9/9 and Ce7/9, and retained
compensation. Conditional compact export stopped at an original-tolerance mismatch in
the fifth model; no six-model compact qualification or promotion.

**Claim boundary.** Three matched A/C instances and exposed panels; one affine
modulation head, unchanged(q,q) training, no explicit zq hint or local memory. No human
confirmation or promotion.

## Provenance rule

Files under `records/` are byte-preserving relocations. Their former paths,
hashes, sizes, and source ref are recorded in `study.toml` and the global
migration map. New interpretation belongs in this capsule or `synthesis/`;
the frozen records themselves are not rewritten.
Commands and relative links inside a frozen report describe its historical
checkout. Use the maintained workflow for current commands, or the snapshot
replay guide for an exact detached-worktree replay.

Add a `figures/` directory only when this study has a promoted, reproducible
study-level figure. Cross-study paper figures belong in `synthesis/figures/`.
