## Scientific role

**Question.** Which factors determine the content, gain, and sensitivity of an episode-local fast-weight write?

**Finding.** Eligibility carries evidence-specific direction, modeled DA has a coarser gain role, and alpha places actual writes in high-gain recurrent directions.

**Claim boundary.** Modeled DA is not identified with Bayesian surprise, and alpha placement is not a unique posterior-direction map.
