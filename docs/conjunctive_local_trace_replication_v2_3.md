# Conjunctive local-trace v2.3 replication

## Conclusion

The unchanged v2.3 persistent conjunctive local trace replicates on both
mandatory independent development backbones, seeds 2102 and 2103. Every
competence and integrity gate passes, and each of the four prospectively
separated primary mechanism links passes independently within each network:

1. retained exact-probability rescue;
2. causal direct rescue;
3. query-address and self-write specificity; and
4. the `P_T`/`L_T` double dissociation.

The registered cross-seed outcome is `replicated_mechanism`. Participants were
bootstrapped separately within each network; participants were never pooled
across networks, and no network-population bootstrap or significance claim was
made. The arithmetic mean of the two point effects is descriptive only.

This result upgrades the following causal decomposition from a one-backbone
candidate to the working project mechanism:

```text
retained encoded evidence -> L_T -> query-addressed direct fidelity
effective relational evidence -> P_T -> remote/global relational assembly
```

It does not establish unique tensor-product coding, biologically separate
stores, network-population prevalence, access to stable-omitted evidence, or a
complete account of human behavior. In particular, the excessive symbolic-
distance slope is not repaired and remains dominated by nonlearned/global
policy. The seed-2101 v2.3 sampled-accuracy failure and historical `0.65`
miss also remain immutable facts of the discovery experiment.

## Behavioral consequences and remaining negatives

The local branch modestly improves sampled learned accuracy in both new
networks:

| Seed | v1 learned | Dual learned | Paired change, 95% bootstrap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2102 | 0.89854 | 0.90341 | +0.00487 [+0.00049, +0.00925] |
| 2103 | 0.91071 | 0.91656 | +0.00584 [+0.00195, +0.00974] |

This secondary endpoint is positive in the two replication seeds, but seed
2101's original `+0.00146 [-0.00244,+0.00536]` failure remains part of the
evidence. Nonlearned sampled accuracy decreases by `-0.00688` in seed 2102 and
`-0.00675` in seed 2103, so overall accuracy also decreases slightly. The
local mechanism is therefore not an unconstrained aggregate gain.

The sampled symbolic-distance slope is not repaired:

| Seed | v1 slope | Dual slope |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2102 | 0.04770 | 0.04772 |
| 2103 | 0.04838 | 0.04864 |

The exact-probability slope is almost unchanged (`0.05976 -> 0.05969` and
`0.05917 -> 0.05913`). Nonlearned queries remain the largest positive source,
contributing `0.04884` and `0.04871` in the two v1 networks; their contribution
increases under the local trace while both learned contributions decrease.
This independently repeats the seed-2101 attribution: direct learned fidelity
and excessive global distance dependence are distinct problems.

Stable omission also remains the main learned-error constraint. Omitted cells
contain `66.65% [57.84%,75.00%]` of v1 exact learned error mass in seed 2102
and `71.33% [62.82%,79.02%]` in seed 2103, while their own local write is
exactly zero. The replicated mechanism solves retained-evidence expression;
it does not yet provide a route by which weak or filtered-out evidence can
persist locally.

## Revised theory and next decisive test

The combined positive evidence now supports one global/local two-state
computational account:

```text
P_T: interacting fast-weight relaxation -> expected-rank-like global assembly
L_T: persistent addressed write -> fidelity to retained direct experience
```

The negative constraint is now evidence access, not storage, query address, or
retained-value expression. A stable-omitted relation has no self write, so the
current local branch cannot act on most remaining learned error. The next
admissible candidate family is therefore a separately registered
`dual_evidence_access_v2.4` test in which the global path retains its frozen
effective-evidence bottleneck while the local path is given a prospectively
defined weak/raw evidence signal. Its exact evidence source, subject-specific
semantics, controls, competence gates, and stop rules must be frozen before
implementation; v2.3 must remain the baseline and no gain may be refit on Liu.

The symbolic-distance discrepancy belongs to a separate `P_T`/nonlearned
policy question. It should not be used to reject the replicated local trace,
and a local-access candidate should not be required to repair it. Conversely,
success on stable-omitted direct pairs would not by itself solve global human
behavior.
