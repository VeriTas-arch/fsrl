## Scientific role

**Question.** Does the frozen cost-constrained RNN still use weakly admitted evidence
for global inference, despite substantially lower connection modification?

**Finding.** All three frozen Cost networks retain beneficial single and joint
weak-evidence contributions, with smaller nonlearned CE effects than Shared. Joint
global weak deletion reduces all-subject Cost order tau from 0.865-0.893 to 0.496-0.512
while preserving registered competence and coherence, at an accuracy cost. Acute routing
evidence; no learned routing or main-model promotion.

**Claim boundary.** Functional causal contribution to terminal readout of frozen
networks, on an already project-exposed synthetic Liu cohort. Not a measurement of
stored bits, absence of coding, a human confirmation, physiological cost or main-model
promotion. Acute joint deletion may be outside the trained distribution.
